Paste doesn't go into my app

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn on Clipdeck. If it is already on, turn it off and on again, then reopen Clipdeck.

The shortcut doesn't open Clipdeck

Another app may use ⇧⌘V. Pick a different shortcut in Clipdeck → Settings → Shortcuts.

Restore my purchase on a new Mac

Settings → License → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where is my data?

Only on your Mac, inside Clipdeck's container. Settings → Advanced → Show Data Folder in Finder.
