Clipdeck

Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Clipdeck does not collect, store on any server, or share any of your data.

What stays on your Mac

Everything you copy while Clipdeck is running is saved only on your Mac, inside Clipdeck's own folder. It is never uploaded. There is no account, no sync and no cloud service.

What Clipdeck ignores

Content that apps mark as confidential (for example passwords from password managers) is not saved. You can exclude any app in Settings → Privacy, pause capture at any time, and clear your history.

Network use

When you copy a web link, Clipdeck may fetch that page's title and preview image to show it on the card. This request goes directly to the website you copied; nothing about you is sent to us. You can turn this off in Settings → General.

Analytics and tracking

Clipdeck has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not track you.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Permissions

Accessibility permission is used only to paste the item you choose into the app you were typing in.

隐私政策（简体中文）

Clipdeck 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。复制的内容只保存在你的 Mac 上，没有账号、同步或云端服务。密码管理器等标记为机密的内容不会被保存。复制网页链接时，Clipdeck 可能直接向该网站获取标题和预览图（可在设置中关闭）。没有统计、广告或第三方 SDK。内购由 Apple 处理。辅助功能权限只用于把你选中的条目贴进当前 App。

隱私權政策（繁體中文）

Clipdeck 不收集、不上傳、不分享你的任何資料。複製的內容只儲存在你的 Mac 上，沒有帳號、同步或雲端服務。密碼管理器等標記為機密的內容不會被儲存。複製網頁連結時，Clipdeck 可能直接向該網站取得標題和預覽圖（可在設定中關閉）。沒有統計、廣告或第三方 SDK。購買由 Apple 處理。輔助使用權限只用於把你選取的項目貼進目前的 App。
